PM RX also indicates errors without our requests:3before and22after. Therefore
this is not restricted to a specific IPbus register. The PM PCS link flag changed
from up to down while GT PLL, TX/RX init and MMCM ready flags were high in every
snapshot. These are sparse status observations, not continuous clock monitoring.

The resident diagnostic counts MAC source.valid & source.error cycles. This is
an aggregate PHY/CRC/datapath error indication, not isolated CRC events, bad-frame
count or BER. Corrupt data could originate at Kasli TX, the link, PM RX or MAC
receive logic. These counters do not identify the defective component.
