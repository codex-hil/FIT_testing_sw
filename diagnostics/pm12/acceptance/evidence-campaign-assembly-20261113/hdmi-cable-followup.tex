\subsection{Interpretation and capture limits}
The 14 reads without a SYS-loss trigger contrast with the previous two triggering
reads. This supports sensitivity to the cable/connector path. It does not establish
that HEC shielding is missing or that MISO crosstalk is the unique cause.
This is a before/after comparison, not controlled A/B/A; contact reseating and
elapsed time are uncontrolled. The clock-loss finding remains open pending
stronger repeat/reversal evidence.

For a non-triggering run the circular buffer retains the final approximately
13 milliseconds of the 100-millisecond capture. The initial SPI frame has been
overwritten. Global extrema and the sticky SYS-loss flag span the armed interval;
the frozen RAM is not a waveform around SPI in these no-trigger captures.
The recovered-clock reference and CDC endpoint limitations still apply.
